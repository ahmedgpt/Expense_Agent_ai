# Subscription Audit Guide

## Steps
1. List recurring charges: same merchant, similar amount, roughly monthly or yearly.
2. Mark each as used weekly, occasionally or never.
3. Cancel or downgrade items not used in 60 days.
4. Look for overlap (several video or music services).
5. Prefer annual plans only for services used consistently; check cancellation terms.

## Free-trial trap
Trials that convert to paid plans are a common source of forgotten charges. Note the trial end date in a reminder.

## Typical impact
Cutting two unused subscriptions of PKR 1,500 each saves PKR 36,000 per year.

## Agent guidance
Cancellation is a consequential action: the agent may recommend it but must never claim to have cancelled anything and must ask the user to do it themselves.
