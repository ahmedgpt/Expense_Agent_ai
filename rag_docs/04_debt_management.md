# Debt Repayment Strategies

## Avalanche method
Pay minimums on all debts, put any extra money on the debt with the highest interest rate. Minimises total interest paid.

## Snowball method
Put extra money on the smallest balance first. Costs more interest but gives quick wins that help motivation.

## Rules
- Never skip minimum payments; late fees and penalty rates compound the problem.
- Credit card revolving balances usually carry the highest rates, so they come first under avalanche.
- Avoid taking new debt to repay old debt unless the new rate is clearly lower and fees are included in the comparison.
- Debt payments above 36% of gross income are generally considered a warning sign.

## Agent guidance
Ask for balance, rate and minimum for each debt before recommending an order. Show both strategies side by side. Do not recommend specific loan products.
