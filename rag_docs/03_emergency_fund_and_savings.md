# Emergency Fund and Savings Goals

## Why
An emergency fund covers job loss, medical bills or urgent repairs without borrowing.

## How much
Common guidance: 3 months of essential expenses for people with stable salaried jobs, 6 months for freelancers, single-income households or dependants. Essential expenses = rent + utilities + groceries + transport + minimum debt payments + insurance.

## Building it
1. Calculate monthly essentials.
2. Set a target (e.g. 3 x essentials).
3. Automate a fixed monthly transfer right after payday.
4. Keep it in an easily accessible, low-risk account separate from daily spending.
5. Rebuild after use before resuming other goals.

## Sinking funds
For predictable irregular costs (annual fees, Eid shopping, laptop replacement) divide the cost by months remaining and save that amount monthly.

## Agent guidance
Compute the target and the months-to-goal from the user's stored savings rate. Present results as estimates and let the user decide.
