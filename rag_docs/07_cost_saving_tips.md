# Practical Ways to Reduce Everyday Spending

## Groceries
Plan weekly meals, buy staples in bulk, compare unit prices and avoid shopping hungry. Typical savings 10-20%.

## Dining out
Set a monthly cap and track it by envelope. Replace two restaurant meals per week with home cooking.

## Transport
Share rides, combine errands, maintain vehicle tyre pressure and service schedule to reduce fuel use.

## Utilities
Switch to LED lighting, service air conditioners before summer, unplug idle devices and use timers on heavy appliances. Compare internet and mobile packages yearly.

## Shopping
Use a 48-hour rule for non-essential purchases above PKR 5,000. Wait for sales for planned purchases only.

## Habit design
Review spending weekly for 10 minutes. Small leaks (daily snacks, impulse apps) add up to large monthly totals.
